Access to Heurist.eu is only available for members of the Heurist Network association, see below.

This function was funded by the Heurist Network association and is only available to members

Heurist Network is a non-profit association which supports the ongoing development and maintenance of Heurist

The association depends entirely on funding provided by membership subscriptions and consultancy

Please:
  • join the association or ask your project, lab or institution to do so.
  • include funding to support Heurist in grant applications and annual budgets.
  • request a quote for any type of work associated with Heurist
    (database setup, website creation, new functionality).
Members of the association and projects which support Heurist financially have access
to a new version of Heurist (version 10) developed by the association:
New capabilities
Improved workflows
Rapid turnaround bug fixes
Compatible with existing databases
Find out about version 10

To discuss membership (including temporary membership while administrative wheels turn),
special requirements or consultancy, please email support@heuristnetwork.org

If you believe that you or your project, lab or institution is a member, but you have not been correctly identified, please email us specifying your database name, user name and institutional affiliation.
